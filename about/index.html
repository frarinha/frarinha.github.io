About

So, you’re a pentester?

Yes, I’m currently working as a Penetration Tester for SwissPost. As a pentester, I’ve helped numerous organizations strengthen their defenses against cyber threats. However, I believe that true risk assessment requires more than just identifying vulnerabilities — it demands a deeper understanding of an organization’s overall security posture. That’s the reason I also like to learn and apply Red Teaming techniques, which provide a more comprehensive and realistic view of an organization’s risk exposure.

Although I tend to gravitate to the offensive side of security, I aspire to explore the blue side of things. For the time being, my main goal is to build a well-rounded perspective on information security operations.
